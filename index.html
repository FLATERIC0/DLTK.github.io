Everyone has
a secret.

DON'T LET THEM KNOW is a social sandbox where your private objectives collide with the group's survival. There is no single right way to play.

01 / Hit the quota

One goal.
Many agendas.

Earn points by completing challenges, delivering items, operating machines, finding valuable objects, taking risks, and helping other players. The quota rises every round.

02 / Play your part

Lie. Deal.
Betray.

Your private objectives might ask you to cooperate, deceive, steal, sabotage, or do something wonderfully suspicious. No one sees your rules but you.

03 / Survive the round

Miss the mark.
You're out.

When the quota fails, players who failed the required conditions are eliminated and sent into spectator mode. The player count keeps changing until only the most adaptable remain.

$ environment --interactive

Explore a giant television studio packed with interactive props, backstage corridors, control rooms, vents, machinery, and restricted areas. Push furniture, hide objects, block entrances—and adapt when blackouts, lockdowns, low gravity, or system errors change the rules.

$ spectator_mode --stay_connected

Spectator mode keeps you in the action. Follow living players, switch between cameras, explore spectator-only spaces, and watch the chaos unfold. You are out of the round—but not out of the story.
